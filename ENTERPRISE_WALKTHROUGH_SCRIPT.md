## 🎯 Pro Tips for Delivering to Company Stakeholders:
1. **Focus on Business Risk Reduction:** Emphasize *governance*, *auditability*, and *preventing hallucinated decisions*.
2. **Highlight the Critic:** In enterprise AI, the biggest fear is LLM unreliability. Showing the Critic actively rejecting a claim is your strongest selling point.
3. **Pacing:** Confident, executive tone. Keep the screen clean with browser full-screen (F11) or zoomed to 110%.

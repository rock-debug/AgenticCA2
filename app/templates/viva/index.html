How Viva Prep Works
1
Full-Text Ingestion

Upload a PDF or paste manuscript text. The system extracts raw text using PyPDF.

2
Semantic Chunking & DB Storage

Text is chunked into 500-word windows with 80-word overlap, tagged with academic sections, and stored in the database.

3
TF-IDF Vector RAG

Queries match against specific chunk vectors, pulling exact paragraphs, formulas, and baseline numbers.

4
Viva Voce Defense & Grilling

Simulates academic committee examinations: generates tough questions, trap questions, and scores your spoken defenses.

Exam Defense Pro Tip

Academic examiners focus on why you chose a specific method, what the underlying assumptions are, and where your approach breaks down. Use the Mock Examiner tab to test your defenses against adversarial questions before your defense!
